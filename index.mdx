## What OxygenDispatch does

OxygenDispatch manages the full lifecycle of medical gas cylinders (tanks) for distributors:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive a batch">
    Register incoming cylinder shipments as **Batches** with supplier info, voucher data, and auto-generated serial numbers. Each batch can contain up to 5,000 tank units.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect and approve">
    Run a **Technical Reception** checklist covering documentary review, certificates of analysis, and physical inspection. Approve or reject individual tanks with anomaly tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage inventory">
    Transfer tanks between **Warehouse Areas** (Reception → Quarantine → Approved Products → Returns) and track every movement in the full audit log.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dispatch to clients">
    Create dispatches by selecting individual tank serials or by specifying a quantity with optional filters. Dispatches automatically record remission details and the client entity type.
  </Step>
</Steps>
